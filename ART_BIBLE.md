# World Forger Art Bible

- Use existing audited vendor assets first.
- Do not use generated placeholder terrain as final art.
- Prefer hero assets and district stamps over huge procedural terrain sheets.
- Fixed art perspective: 2.5D RPG/top-down angled.
- Gameplay camera may zoom/pan, but no rotation.
- Whisperwind palette: blue/teal water, warm windows, green foliage, stone paths, wood docks.
- Any new generated asset must be a single transparent PNG or exact-grid sheet. No labels, no posters, no UI.
