# Hero Lanes Drop-in

This adds a playable first version of a lane-based upgrade RPG side game inspired by the feel of old custom-map lane grinders, without copying names, heroes, code, balance, or assets.

## Files

- `games/hero_lanes.html` — standalone playable game.
- `games.json` — adds Hero Lanes to the launcher.

## Current Gameplay

- Pick one of 5 hero classes.
- Click one of 3 lanes: Forest Path, Mountain Pass, Shadow Road.
- Your hero auto-fights waves and bosses.
- Earn XP and gold.
- Buy battle upgrades: damage, health, regen, crit, attack speed.
- Rebirth after level 15+ for souls.
- Spend souls on permanent upgrades.
- Progress saves locally in the browser under `gameforge_hero_lanes_v1`.

## Notes

This is intentionally a fast playable prototype. Next logical upgrades are server-side saves, multiplayer shared lanes, hero skills, item drops, boss timers, lane upgrade tiers, leaderboards, and visual asset replacement.
