World Boundary Editor

Simple workflow:
1. Click Load Shadow Woods.
2. Pick a layer and draw polygons/circles.
3. Drag white dots to reshape.
4. Export/download JSON.
5. Replace public/assets/worlds/shadow_woods_dock.json in GitHub.
