# Whisperwind Build Plan

## Goal

Whisperwind should feel like an exploratory RPG hub, not a flat map. The first map must contain:

- stairwells,
- alleys,
- enterable buildings,
- a claimable home,
- a tavern,
- a pet center,
- future arcade/minigame hook,
- dock/fishing hook,
- multiple town levels.

## Districts in v2 hub

1. Lower Town / Residential Row
2. Market Square
3. Tavern Lane
4. Upper Cliff Homes
5. Hidden Alley
6. Dockside

## What makes this closer to FF6-style town design

The player cannot understand everything from one flat overview. The map is now designed as:

Lower town → residential row → market square → tavern terrace → upper stair → cliff homes, with side paths to the hidden alley and docks.

## Avoid

- giant filled road rectangles,
- open empty grass,
- asset showcase layouts,
- buildings painted into one background image,
- houses without doors/interiors.
