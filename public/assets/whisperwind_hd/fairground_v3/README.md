# Two-mouth grotto

`two_mouth_grotto.png` is original transparent pixel art for the Lantern Run tunnel. It has a distinct right entrance and left exit, joined by one rocky ridge. The artwork is drawn at 420×225 world units around the tunnel anchor in `scripts/build_whisperwind_expanded.py`; both rail ends run through the openings and the cart hides while under the rock.

The station approach and BOARD marker are also authored by that script. Keep the returning track east of the station's roof, the dock at its right platform edge, and the boarding hotspot on the public road. Regenerate the expanded and default scene JSONs after changes and verify boarding from the fair spawn in the browser.
